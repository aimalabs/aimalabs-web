The platform

A software-only AI platform for the blood smear.

Hardware-independent, microscope-agnostic, and built as assistive decision support — the specialist always confirms the result.

The workflow

From slide to signed-off, in four steps.

STEP 01

Capture

Photograph or upload the stained smear straight from the microscope camera — no proprietary scanner required.

STEP 02

AI analysis

The model classifies cells and quantifies morphology across the blood-cell classes in under two minutes.

STEP 03

Structured output

White blood cell morphology, ICSH-aligned red cell morphology grading and platelet estimation, presented clearly.

STEP 04

Expert confirms

A pathologist or haematologist reviews and signs off. The AI supports the specialist; it never decides alone.

What AIMALABS sees

From a field of cells to a structured readout.

On each smear, AIMALABS localises and classifies cells across the blood-cell classes, grades red-cell morphology to ICSH conventions, and estimates platelets — turning a microscope image into a consistent, reviewable result in under two minutes.

Two models, one platform

Built for the lab and the point of care.

AIMALABS runs as two variants of a single architecture: a full laboratory model for deployment in the lab, and a lightweight model for decentralised, point-of-care use — so the same standardised analysis follows the patient wherever the smear is taken.
