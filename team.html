A true story

Meet Sofia. Tired, pale, always breathless. For seven years no one could explain why — the blood smear was never checked. Then one smear changed everything: her red cells were spherical and fragile, a classic sign of Hereditary Spherocytosis.

Today Sofia is thriving. Millions like her are still waiting. An answer that takes two minutes should never take seven years — that gap is why AIMALABS exists.
